## vstg-0081 — Treaty of Sèvres, 1920

**Código visible:** vstg.1920  ·  **Nivel:** documented  ·  **Estado:** Verificada con Wikipedia: firmado el 10 de agosto de 1920 en Sèvres; sustituido por el Tratado de Lausana (1923); nunca ratificado.

**[desc.]** The peace treaty signed on 10 August 1920 between the Allies and the Ottoman government. It partitioned most of the empire, set up an independent Armenia and a referendum for Kurdistan, and gave Smyrna to Greek administration.

**[f.f. expl.]** An Anatolia split between mandates, zones and small states, with a Kurdistan that might have existed. The Turkish national movement rejected the treaty and won the war; the borders were redrawn at Lausanne in 1923.

## vstg-0083 — Acheson–Lilienthal Report, 1946

**Código visible:** vstg.1946.a  ·  **Nivel:** documented  ·  **Estado:** Verificada con Wikipedia: informe fechado en marzo de 1946, propiedad internacional del material fisible.

**[desc.]** A 1946 report on the international control of atomic energy, drafted largely by J. Robert Oppenheimer for a committee led by Dean Acheson and David Lilienthal. It proposed that all fissile material be owned by an international authority.

**[f.f. expl.]** A world in which no nation could build the bomb, because the uranium itself belonged to everyone. Presented to the UN in a weakened version, it was rejected in 1946, and the nuclear race began.

**Fuente:** Acheson, D. and Lilienthal, D. (1946). A Report on the International Control of Atomic Energy. Wikipedia, "Acheson–Lilienthal Report".
