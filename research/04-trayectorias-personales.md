## vstg-0009 — Len Bias, unused jersey no. 30

**Año (código):** 1986  ·  **Nivel:** documented  ·  **Estado:** Investigada: duración de las condenas y su reforma por verificar

**[desc.]** The number 30 Boston Celtics jersey that Len Bias never wore. He was chosen second overall in the 1986 NBA draft and died two days later, at 22.

**[f.f. expl.]** A career the press already compared to Michael Jordan's. His death in June 1986 became the face of a national alarm about cocaine. Within months Congress passed the Anti-Drug Abuse Act, known as the Len Bias law, which set mandatory minimum sentences that stayed in force for decades. The future that failed was a player's; the one that followed belonged to people who never knew him.

**Fuente:** Lassen, F. (2015). "A Cracked Remedy: The Anti-Drug Abuse Act". Temple Law Review 87: 645.
